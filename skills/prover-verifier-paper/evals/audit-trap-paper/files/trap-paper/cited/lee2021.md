# Verbalized confidence and overconfidence in question answering models
Jae Lee, Min Kim, Ana Ortiz. 2021. Proceedings of the Imaginary Workshop on Model Confidence.

## Abstract
We study whether the confidence that question-answering models state in words tracks their accuracy.
Across four model sizes (1B, 3B, 7B and 13B parameters) evaluated on TriviaQA, we observe a positive
correlation between verbalized confidence and error rate for models with at least 7B parameters; for the
1B and 3B models the relationship was not statistically significant. Our observational design does not
permit causal conclusions about whether eliciting verbal confidence produces overconfidence.

## 6 Limitations
All results are correlational and limited to a single dataset (TriviaQA).
